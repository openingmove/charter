# Cinnamon Buns

An enriched dough rolled with butter, sugar and cinnamon.

## Method

Make a soft dough with 500 g flour, milk, butter and yeast. Roll thin, spread with the filling, roll up and cut into twelve. Bake for 18 minutes.
