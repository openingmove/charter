# Milk Loaf

A soft sandwich loaf. Retired in 2025 when the supplier changed (see [[Use one flour supplier]]); the Focaccia now covers the same everyday niche.

## Method

Mix flour, warm milk, salt and yeast into a soft dough, prove twice and bake in a tin for 35 minutes.
