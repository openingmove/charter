# Seeded Crackers

Thin, crisp crackers made from leftover dough scraps, oats and seeds.

## Method

Roll the dough paper thin, brush with water, scatter with oats and seeds and bake until golden, about 12 minutes.

## Notes

Only made when there is spare dough, so hard to test on a schedule.
