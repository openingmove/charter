# Rye Bread

A dense, slightly sour loaf. Mix 400 g rye flour with 200 g bread flour, 12 g salt and 7 g yeast, then add 450 g warm water.

## Method

Rest the dough for one hour, shape, and prove for 45 minutes. Bake in the [Deck Oven](../equipment/Deck%20Oven.md) for 40 minutes.
