# Plain Baguette

Long, crackly loaves with an open crumb.

## Method

A wet dough with a long cold ferment. Shape gently so as not to knock out the bubbles, and bake with plenty of steam in the [[Deck Oven]].

## Notes

Needs a higher hydration than the others. Comes out well roughly one bake in three.
