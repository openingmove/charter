# Focaccia

A forgiving, wet dough baked in an oiled tray and dimpled with fingertips before it goes in.

## Method

Mix 500 g flour, 400 g water, 10 g salt and 4 g yeast. Rest for three hours with two folds. Bake in the [[Deck Oven]] for 22 minutes.

## Notes

A good first recipe for new staff.
