# Sourdough Boule

Our flagship loaf. 500 g bread flour, 350 g water, 100 g active starter and 10 g salt.

## Method

Fold the dough four times over the first two hours, shape, and let it prove in the fridge overnight. Bake in the [[Deck Oven]] with steam for 20 minutes, then without for 25.

## Notes
