# Test new recipes on Mondays

New recipes are only trial-baked on Mondays, when the shop is closed and the [[Deck Oven]] is free. A bake is logged the same day, in the bakes folder, with an outcome of good, ok or poor.

This keeps experiments away from trading days, so a failed batch never costs a customer a loaf.
