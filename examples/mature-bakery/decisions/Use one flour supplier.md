# Use one flour supplier

We buy all our flour from [[Millstone Farm]]. Mixing flours from several mills made our loaves vary from week to week, and a single supplier lets us order in bulk and learn how its flour behaves in the dough.

The cost is that we depend on one mill. If it ever cannot deliver, the fallback is to buy bread flour from [Harbour Wholesale](../suppliers/Harbour%20Wholesale.md) for a single week.
