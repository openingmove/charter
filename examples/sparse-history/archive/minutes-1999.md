# Minutes of the committee, 1999

Meeting of 8 February. Present: seven members. Balance 188 pounds. The dehumidifier has done its work and the ledgers are dry.

Meeting of 10 May. A member proposed recording the memories of older residents. Agreed in principle; nobody yet owns a tape recorder.

Meeting of 13 September. The regatta had thirty-one boats, a record. The committee noted its thanks to the organisers.

Meeting of 6 December. The society agreed to keep these minutes in the archive folder as they stand, unedited, for the record.
