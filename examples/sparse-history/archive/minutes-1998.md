# Minutes of the committee, 1998

Meeting of 9 February. Present: six members. The treasurer reported a balance of 212 pounds. It was agreed to ask the council about opening the reading room on Saturday mornings.

Meeting of 11 May. The regatta committee reported a surplus of 40 pounds from last year's event and asked whether the society wished to co-sponsor it again. Agreed, with thanks.

Meeting of 14 September. The tea room on Quay Street opened in July and has been supplying refreshments for the Saturday tours. The secretary was asked to write and say thank you.

Meeting of 7 December. The cabinet in the reading room was found damp. The treasurer was authorised to spend up to 60 pounds on a dehumidifier.
