# Swarming

## Why it matters

A swarm takes a large share of a hive's workforce away at the start of the honey season. Knowing the signs lets a keeper split the colony on purpose instead.

## Examples

Queen cells along the bottom of the frames, and a crowded brood box in the weeks of a strong [[Nectar flow]].
